About this page

This webpage presents a clean and responsive Vaishno Devi image gallery built for publishing on prashant.dhingra.website. Every image includes descriptive alt text and a responsive srcset so visitors receive the most suitable version based on their device and screen size.

It also includes search-engine-friendly metadata, Open Graph and Twitter tags for social sharing, and structured JSON-LD data to help search engines and generative systems understand the gallery content more effectively.
