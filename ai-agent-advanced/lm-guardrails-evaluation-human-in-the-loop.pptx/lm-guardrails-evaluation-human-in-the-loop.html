Route decisions by risk, not a single confidence score

Read slide 8 text
L L M A P P L I C AT I O N A S S U R A N C E Route decisions by risk, not a single confidence score LLM governance architecture • Practical reference design 08 RISK TIER EXAMPLE CONDITIONS SYSTEM RESPONSE ASSURANCE LOW Read-only, reversible Automate Log + sample evaluation MEDIUM Ambiguous or sensitive Constrain / ask clarification Targeted review or second check HIGH External effects, regulated or irreversible Hold for approval Named approver + evidence + audit BLOCK Disallowed policy or permission Deny safely Incident signal as appropriate Confidence is useful as one signal; pair it with impact, permissions, evidence, and policy.
