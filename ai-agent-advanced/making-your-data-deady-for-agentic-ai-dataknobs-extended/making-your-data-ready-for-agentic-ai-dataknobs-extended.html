SLIDE 13 / 38

Freshness, streaming and temporal correctness

Read slide 13 text
D ATA R EAD I N E S S / AG E N TI C A I Freshness, streaming and temporal correctness 13 • MAKING YOUR DATA READY FOR AGENTIC AI Define per-use-case freshness: a policy lookup may tolerate days; a payment balance may not. Record event time, processing time, effective dates and timezone explicitly. Choose streaming or change data capture where latency materially changes the decision. Provide “as-of” timestamps and stale-data behavior in every agent tool response. Reconcile late-arriving events and invalidate caches when source data changes. Freshness is a contractual property of the data product—not an assumption made by the agent.

Production readiness checklist

Read slide 26 text
D ATA R EAD I N E S S / AG E N TI C A I Production readiness checklist 26 • MAKING YOUR DATA READY FOR AGENTIC AI Named owners and versioned contracts exist for every agent-facing data product. Certified semantics, valid-time interpretation, quality and freshness are defined. Source, index, cache, tool, memory and trace permissions are consistent. Golden evaluations cover discovery, retrieval, analytics, security and outcomes. Tool actions are scoped, audited, rate-limited and reversible where possible. Human approvals, incident response, rollback and deprecation paths are tested. Agent-ready = discoverable + meaningful + trusted + permissioned + observable + evaluable.
