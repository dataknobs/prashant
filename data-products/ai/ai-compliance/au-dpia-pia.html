Instead of manual checklists, the AI analyzes the combination of Data Types and Processing Purposes to predict risk vectors instantly. It maps these against GDPR/CCPA requirements to calculate a dynamic risk score.

MODULE B Automated DPIA Generation

By leveraging Generative AI, the system can pre-fill up to 80% of a DPIA draft based on system architecture and defined parameters. This shifts the compliance officer's role from "writer" to "reviewer."
