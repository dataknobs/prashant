Automate the Unmanageable

By Prashant Dhingra | Governance, Quality & Privacy

Transform regulatory compliance from a reactive, manual burden into a proactive, AI-driven advantage. Explore how AI automates Records of Processing Activities (RoPA), Evidence Collection, and Policy Monitoring.

Manual vs. AI-Driven Compliance

📂 Module A

Records of Processing Activities (RoPA)

The Challenge: Maintaining an accurate inventory of how data flows through your organization usually requires annual surveys that are outdated the moment they are filed.

The AI Solution: Auto-extraction of processing details from system logs and documentation allows for continuous updates instead of manual reporting.

Regulatory Reporting & Evidence

Mapping internal controls to the alphabet soup of regulations (GDPR, CCPA, CPRA, LGPD) is complex. AI automates evidence gathering for audits and identifies gaps instantly.

Policy Sentinel
