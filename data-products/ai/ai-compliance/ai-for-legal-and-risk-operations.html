Contract & Vendor Risk Analysis

AI systems act as a 24/7 legal associate, reviewing DPAs (Data Processing Agreements), SCCs, and vendor contracts. They automatically detect non-standard privacy clauses and perform continuous monitoring of third-party risk profiles.
