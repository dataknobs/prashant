The unique idea: Knobs

Data platforms typically store, move, and retrieve data. DataKnobs specializes in identifying and implementing the most influential controls that drive business results - the knobs you may not even realize you have.

Core concept

What is a knob?

Definition

A factor that can be controlled, such as a signal, configuration, or decision parameter, that has a significant impact on business results

Many companies struggle with managing numerous variables, lacking insight into their importance, relationships, and the need for continuous optimization. This is where DataKnobs excels.

How knobs relate to data products

Data products provide context. Knobs provide control.

Both are crucial. Data products reveal the truth, while knobs dictate adjustments.

The DataKnobs flywheel

A much more sophisticated loop than a generic "AI platform"

Operations create signals, which are transformed into data products that reveal knobs, driving optimization and leading to feedback on outcomes, which in turn refine the knobs.

From categories to coordinates

Knobs are coordinates in a control geometry

The five categories describe what knobs areAs soon as you begin adjusting them, knobs act as if they are coordinates on a curved plane - some are separate, some are connected, some hold more significant details about operation. This is where the intelligence of knobs comes into play.

Orthogonal dimensions

Independent knobs

Certain knobs control behavior along separate axes, with adjustments to one having no impact on the others. These knobs are considered high-leverage controls, as even minor adjustments can result in distinct and precise effects.

Correlated dimensions

Coupled knobs

The movement of one knob affects others, as they are interconnected. Smart knob technology recognizes these connections to prevent wasted effort.

High-information dimensions

Frontier knobs

EKIP focuses on fine-tuning the select knobs that reside on the edge of system behavior, where even minor adjustments can lead to significant changes in outcomes.

This is what we mean by control geometry the knobs' structure serves as the technical foundation for EKIP to accurately adjust behavior change directions, avoiding the need to guess one slider at a time. Read the mathematics →
