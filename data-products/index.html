Why CDO/CAIO/CPO focus on Data Product

A Chief Data Officer should focus on data products because they convert data from a managed asset into a revenue-generating and decision-enabling capability. By adopting a data product operating model, the CDO can drive domain accountability, improve data quality at the source, reduce duplication across teams, and accelerate time-to-value. This approach also strengthens cross-functional alignment with business, technology, and product leaders, positioning the CDO not as a custodian of data, but as a strategic enabler of enterprise intelligence and growth.

Why Agentic AI need Data Products

Data products serve as the foundation for AI and agentic systems, offering a reliable framework for LLMs, robust feature stores for ML models, effective retrieval layers for RAG architectures, and enforceable data contracts for real-time decision-making. In the realm of agentic AI, stable and clearly defined data products empower autonomous agents to think, act, and adapt workflows independently.
