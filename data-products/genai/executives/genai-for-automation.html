Generative AI in
Automation.

By Prashant Dhingra | Generative AI & LLMs

Moving beyond simple rule-based bots. Generative AI unlocks cognitive automation, allowing systems to read, write, summarize, and orchestrate unstructured workflows that previously required human intelligence.

View Use Cases RPA vs GenAI

How GenAI Drives Automation

Generative AI acts as a reasoning engine, capable of taking over tasks that involve synthesizing information, drafting content, and parsing complex documents.

Unstructured Data Processing

Extracting entities, clauses, and structured JSON data directly from messy PDFs, contracts, emails, and invoices without relying on rigid OCR templates.

Customer Support Ops

Automating complex customer inquiries by instantly synthesizing internal knowledge bases, drafting contextual emails, and routing tickets based on sentiment.

Software & IT Automation

Accelerating development by generating boilerplate code, automatically documenting legacy systems, and summarizing complex IT alert logs into actionable steps.

Content Orchestration

Automating the drafting of marketing copy, personalized sales outreach, and internal reporting by aggregating data from various enterprise silos.

Paradigm Shift

Traditional RPA vs. Cognitive Automation

For years, Robotic Process Automation (RPA) was the gold standard. However, RPA breaks the moment a process deviates from rigid, predictable rules or encounters unstructured data.

Generative AI bridges this gap. By acting as a reasoning layer, it can handle exceptions, interpret nuance, and parse unpredictable formats vastly expanding the surface area of what an enterprise can automate.

Traditional RPA

  • • Strictly "If This, Then That" rules.
  • • Requires highly structured, tabular data.
  • • Fails when UI interfaces or templates change.
  • • Simulates human clicks and keystrokes.

GenAI Automation

  • • Evaluates context and "reasons" through exceptions.
  • • Thrives on unstructured text, images, and emails.
  • • Highly adaptable to changing inputs or instructions.
  • • Simulates human comprehension and drafting.
