From Followers to Relevance

By Prashant Dhingra | Use Cases & Industry

The balance of power has shifted from follower count to content value due to modern algorithms.

The New Reality: Being "big" was the advantage. Now, being useful or interesting is the advantage.

Understanding the Paradigm Shift

The landscape of social media has undergone a fundamental shift. The traditional strategy of building a large audience for automatic reach is no longer effective. Today, algorithms prioritize relevance, engagement, and value rather than raw follower counts. This change is one of the most significant in digital marketing over the last decade.

In 2024 and beyond, it is vital for individuals constructing an audience, producing content, or implementing social media strategies to comprehend this transition.

📊 The Old Paradigm

  • More followers = more visibility
  • Growth was the primary objective
  • Big accounts always won
  • Reach was predictable
  • Follower count was the status symbol
  • Algorithm boosted established creators
  • Saturation less of an issue

✨ The New Paradigm

  • Relevance beats follower count
  • Engagement quality matters most
  • Small accounts can go viral
  • Content is tested with strangers first
  • Value decides distribution
  • Algorithm rewards relevance
  • Creator economy is democratized

What Changed? Understanding the Algorithm Evolution

The Three Major Shifts

1

From Follower-Based Distribution to Algorithm-Based Distribution

Platforms no longer display content to all followers; algorithms now assess engagement, relevance, and quality to decide which posts are shown to specific users.

2

From Predictable Reach to Dynamic Reach

A post from a 500k follower account no longer guarantees reaching 100k people; instead, reach is determined by the algorithm's assessment of content quality, initial engagement, and relevance to the target audience.

3

From Centralized Discovery to Distributed Discovery

Users are now discovering content from new creators through algorithms that prioritize freshness and relevance over follower count, instead of relying solely on following accounts.

💡 The democratization of social media has led to a significant change. With just 1,000 followers, a creator has the potential to reach millions by delivering valuable content.

The Four Pillars of the New Paradigm

🎯

Relevance Over Reach

Relevance of content to specific audience segments is assessed, with a smaller engaged audience proving more effective than a larger disengaged one.

💡

Value Is Currency

Information that educates, entertains, or inspires is given a boost. The algorithm determines if it is valuable or engaging to individuals.

🧪

Testing with Strangers

Each post undergoes a test with non-followers first. If they interact, the algorithm broadens the reach; if not, the reach remains limited.

🚀

Viral Potential Everywhere

Quality content, not follower count, is now the key factor in determining whether small accounts can go viral.

How Modern Algorithms Work

The Engagement-First Model

When you make a post, modern platforms prioritize engagement signals to decide how widely it gets distributed.

1

Initial Testing Phase

Your content is displayed to a small, diverse group of users, not limited to just your followers, and the algorithm observes their responses.

2

Engagement Evaluation

The algorithm tracks engagement metrics like likes, comments, shares, saves, and watch time, as well as negative indicators such as mutes, unfollows, and skips.

3

Relevance Scoring

The relevance of your content is determined by its alignment with audience interests, current trends, and user behavior.

4

Distribution Expansion

Positive scores lead to an exponential expansion of distribution. Content with high engagement has the potential to reach millions, regardless of your number of followers.

What Gets Amplified?

  • High Engagement Content: Posts that generate genuine, meaningful engagement such as comments, shares, and saves are prioritized over passive views in ranking.
  • Original Insights: Unique perspectives and original information outperform reposts and generic content
  • Audience Resonance: Content that sparks genuine conversations and emotions gets prioritized
  • Consistency: Consistent quality and regular posting by creators result in improved algorithmic support.
  • Native Content: Original content specifically designed for the platform outperforms content that is repurposed from elsewhere.
  • Trending Relevance: Content aligned with current conversations and trends gets algorithmic boosts

What Gets Suppressed?

  • Low Engagement: Posts lacking meaningful engagement will gradually reach a smaller audience.
  • Misleading Content: Misinformation and clickbait trigger algorithmic penalties
  • Over-Promotion: Heavy-handed sales content gets deprioritized in favor of authentic engagement
  • Controversial/Hateful Content: Posts violating community standards are limited or hidden
  • Repetitive Content: Similar posts in quick succession may trigger spam filters
  • External Links (Platform Dependent): Certain platforms may penalize posts that contain external links in order to safeguard their users within the platform.

What This Means for Content Creators

Challenges of the New Era

Challenge 1: Growth Doesn't Equal Reach

Gaining 10,000 new followers no longer guarantees increased reach. Audience access is no longer guaranteed with follower growth.

Challenge 2: Constant Pressure to Innovate

The algorithm encourages innovation. Past success does not guarantee current results as audiences crave new, unique material.

Challenge 3: Quality Over Quantity

Consistently posting subpar content can harm results, as one high-quality post outperforms several average ones.

Challenge 4: Niche Matters More Than Size

Content that is broad and generic tends to have difficulty gaining traction, while content that is highly specific and resonates deeply with a smaller audience tends to perform better.

Opportunities in the New Paradigm

Opportunity 1: Level Playing Field

Emerging creators have the potential to rival established ones. Content from lesser-known accounts can quickly gain popularity and attract large followings in no time.

Opportunity 2: Authenticity Premium

Real, genuine voices hold more weight than polished corporate language. Showing personality and vulnerability can give you a competitive edge.

Opportunity 3: Community-Driven Growth

Focusing on building engaged communities is more important than pursuing meaningless numbers. True supporters help spread your content by sharing and commenting.

Opportunity 4: Niche Authority

Achieving expert status in a particular niche holds greater value than being a generalist with a wide following.

Practical Strategies for the Relevance-First Era

1. Focus on Engagement Quality, Not Vanity Metrics

  • Stop obsessing over follower count. Instead, monitor meaningful interactions such as authentic comments, shares, saves, and video completion rates.
  • Create conversation-starters. Ask questions, seek opinions, and encourage authentic discussion in comments
  • Respond to every comment. The algorithm rewards engagement and meaningful interaction in comment sections

2. Understand Your Specific Audience

  • Identify your true niche. Who specifically benefits from your content? Get specific, not broad
  • Research audience pain points. Create content addressing specific challenges your audience faces
  • Test and iterate. Diverse content resonates with diverse audiences; ongoing testing helps identify what connects.

3. Create Value-First Content

  • Educate, entertain, or inspire. Each post should provide clear value to your audience
  • Original insights matter. Share unique perspectives and original research, not regurgitated content
  • Quality over frequency. One excellent post performs better than three mediocre ones

4. Leverage Algorithm Mechanics

  • Hook users immediately. First 3 seconds are critical; grab attention right away
  • Optimize for retention. Watch time and completion rates are key engagement signals
  • Call to action strategically. Encourage saves, shares, and comments::these are strong engagement signals

5. Build Authentic Community

  • Be genuinely you. Authenticity resonates; personas and over-editing don't
  • Show behind-the-scenes. Audiences connect with real people, not polished brands
  • Engage with your community daily. Respond to messages, comments, and engage with others' content

6. Consistency with Purpose

  • Post on a schedule. Consistency helps the algorithm understand your content pattern
  • Maintain quality standards. Consistency without quality actually hurts performance
  • Stay on-brand. A cohesive content focus outperforms random, unfocused content

Case Studies: Relevance in Action

Case Study 1: The Unknown Expert

Situation: A software engineer who has 2,000 followers began posting brief video tutorials on coding tips.

What Happened: A single video simplifying a complicated idea struck a chord, garnering 5 million views and causing their follower count to triple within two weeks, all thanks to algorithmic promotion.

Key Learning: Quality is more important than quantity when it comes to social media. The algorithm prioritizes content that connects with users, regardless of follower count.

Case Study 2: The Micro-Creator

Situation: A UX designer who has 500 devoted followers crafted in-depth critiques of well-known apps.

What Happened: Despite having over 100k followers, many struggled to even reach 5k views, while their highly targeted content consistently reached over 200k people, establishing them as the top choice for design professionals.

Key Learning: Having expertise in a specific area is more valuable than having a wide audience. Providing valuable content to a targeted audience is more effective than providing generic content to a large audience.

Case Study 3: The Authenticity Win

Situation: A business profile boasting 100k followers shared professional, sales-oriented posts yet experienced a decrease in audience interaction.

What Happened: Despite not gaining any new followers, engagement doubled when they shifted to sharing genuine behind-the-scenes content featuring real team members.

Key Learning: Authenticity is key. The algorithm favors and boosts raw, genuine content instead of polished, artificial content.

Common Mistakes in the New Era

Mistake 1: Buying Followers

Artificial followers can significantly decrease your engagement rate, which is a crucial indicator in algorithms. An account boasting 100k genuine followers will outperform another account with 100k followers primarily consisting of bots.

Mistake 2: Posting Constantly Without Purpose

Posting too frequently without maintaining quality can negatively impact performance. The algorithm recognizes that low engagement indicates your content is not valuable. Focus on quality over quantity by posting less frequently but with higher quality content.

Mistake 3: Ignoring Analytics

Stop speculating on what is effective and instead, delve into analytics: analyze which posts are saved, which generate comments, and which keep viewers engaged the longest. Let data guide your strategic decisions.

Mistake 4: Being Too Salesy

Algorithms tend to suppress overly promotional content. Audiences prefer valuable assistance over constant sales pitches.

Mistake 5: Inconsistency

Inconsistent posting can lead to confusion for both the algorithm and your audience. Even just two posts per week can greatly improve performance.

Mistake 6: Not Adapting to Platform Differences

What may be successful on TikTok may not yield the same results on LinkedIn. It's important to adapt your strategy to fit the unique algorithms, cultures, and user preferences of each platform.

The Future: What's Next?

Predicted Trends

  • AI-Powered Personalization: Algorithms will get even better at tailoring content to personal preferences.
  • Community Monetization: Creators will have increased opportunities to earn directly from their engaged communities through various platforms.
  • Creator Transparency: We will be provided with additional tools to better understand the reasons behind the content recommendations you receive.
  • Niche Networks: Anticipate the rise of niche social platforms tailored to specific groups (evidenced by Discord and Substack).
  • Direct Creator-Audience Relationships: Platforms for creators to connect with audiences independently of algorithms (email, Discord, newsletters)
  • Emphasis on First-Party Data: Creators who possess audience data, such as email lists and membership communities, will enjoy a competitive edge.

What Stays Constant

Despite algorithmic changes, some fundamentals remain:

  • Value always wins. Content that genuinely helps people will continue to perform well
  • Authenticity resonates. Real, genuine voices will always outperform fake personas
  • Community matters. Building real relationships with your audience compounds over time
  • Consistency pays off. Showing up regularly builds trust and algorithmic support

Ready to Shift Your Strategy?

The time of fixating on followers is past. Focus on staying relevant, authentic, and providing real value. The quicker you adjust, the more advantage you'll have.
