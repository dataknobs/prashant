Welcome to the Interactive Dussehra Explorer. Dussehra (synonymous with Vijayadashami or Dasha-hara) ranges from royal pageants in South India to 75-day tribal covenants in Chhattisgarh, environmental promenade theaters in Uttar Pradesh, and indentured heritage preservation across the Americas and Pacific. Festival Duration Comparison

Number of continuous calendar days dedicated to rituals and public festivities

Insight: Bastar Dussehra (75 days) and Ramnagar Ramlila (31 days) represent deep temporal devotions, whereas Western diaspora celebrations condense rituals into intensive 1-day stadium melas.

Theological Paradigm Distribution

Categorization by core divine archetype and liturgical focus

Insight: While Northern India and the Caribbean Girmitiya diaspora are predominantly Vaishnavite (Rama), South/East India and Nepal lean strongly Shakta (Durga/Kali), alongside unique tribal traditions.

Historical Epoch & Origins

Distribution across historical eras of establishment

Insight: Celebrations reflect distinct historical waves: ancient temple rites, 14th-18th century royal statecraft, 19th-century indentured Girmitiya migrations, and late 20th-century Western diaspora hubs.

Geographic Dispersion

Count of landmark sites by geographical territory

Insight: Exactly 40% (8 of 20) of the surveyed landmark Dussehra traditions flourish outside mainland India, illustrating global cultural transmission across oceans and continents.

Thematic Taxonomy & Comparative Matrix. Dussehra cannot be reduced to a single mythological storyline. Examine the cross-cutting matrix below detailing focal divinities, core rituals, duration, historical origins, and core socio-political functions.

PARADIGM 1

Vaishnavite / Heroic

Focal Archetype: Lord Rama (*Ramcharitmanas*)
Core Theme: Ethical sovereignty (*Maryada Purushottam*), triumph of righteousness over pride/ego (*Ravana*).
Primary Geography: North/West India, Gangetic Plain, Caribbean & Pacific Girmitiya diaspora.

PARADIGM 2

Shakta / Cosmic Feminine

Focal Archetype: Durga / Chamundeshwari / Kali / Mutharamman
Core Theme: Primordial female energy annihilating Mahishasura; subduing ego; homecomings.
Primary Geography: Bengal, South India (Karnataka, Tamil Nadu), Nepal, Mauritius.

PARADIGM 3

Indigenous / Non-Epic

Focal Archetype: Devi Danteshwari, Local Devtas, Nature Covenants
Core Theme: Inter-valley divine assemblies, tribal clan integration, ecological pacts, no effigy burnings.
Primary Geography: Bastar tribal belt, Kullu mountain valley.
