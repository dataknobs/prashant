Diwali 2026 muhurat in short

In short

Diwali 2026 is on Sunday 8 November in every country listed here. Lakshmi Puja is done in Pradosh Kaal with the fixed Vrishabha lagna, so the clock time changes by city: 5:54 PM – 7:50 PM in New Delhi, 6:27 PM – 8:27 PM in Mumbai, 4:34 PM – 6:12 PM in London, 5:02 PM – 6:50 PM in New York and 8:05 PM – 9:33 PM in Sydney. What each window means

  • Main window

    Pradosh Kaal

    The first fifth of the night, from local sunset to the next sunrise divided by five. It is about two and a half hours long in November and is the main window for Lakshmi Puja.

  • Fixed lagna

    Vrishabha Kaal

    The time when Vrishabha (Taurus) rises. It is a sthir (fixed) lagna, and worship in a fixed lagna is believed to make Lakshmi stay. The best muhurat is where it overlaps Pradosh Kaal.

  • The date

    Amavasya tithi

    Lakshmi Puja must fall inside Kartik Amavasya. In 2026 it runs from 11:27 AM IST on 8 November to 12:31 PM IST on 9 November, which covers the evening of the 8th everywhere on this page.

  • Night puja

    Mahanishita Kaal

    The muhurta at the middle of the night, used for Nishita puja, tantric worship and Kali Puja in Bengal. On the west coast of North America, Amavasya ends before it.

  • Night lagna

    Simha Kaal

    Simha (Leo), another fixed lagna, rises around midnight in India and the northern hemisphere. Some families choose it for the night puja.

  • Business muhurat

    Choghadiya

    Day and night are each split into eight parts. Shubh, Labh, Amrit and Chal are auspicious; traders often use them for opening new account books. The finder lists those that fall within Amavasya.

How the muhurat is calculated

Every window on this page is calculated for the exact place, not copied from Delhi and shifted by time zone.

  1. Sunset and sunriseThe sun's position is computed for the city's latitude and longitude, with sunset when its upper edge meets the horizon (−0.833°, allowing for refraction).
  2. Pradosh KaalThe night from sunset to the next sunrise is divided into five; the first part is Pradosh Kaal. Mahanishita is the eighth of fifteen night muhurtas.
  3. Vrishabha and Simha lagnaThe rising sign is computed from local sidereal time and converted to the sidereal zodiac with the Lahiri ayanamsa used by Indian panchangs.
  4. TithiTrayodashi and Amavasya begin and end at the same moment everywhere; the page converts those instants to local time and keeps only the parts of each window inside the tithi.
  5. MuhuratLakshmi Puja muhurat is where Pradosh Kaal, Vrishabha Kaal and Amavasya overlap. If Vrishabha does not rise during Pradosh Kaal somewhere, the page says so and shows the Pradosh window.

Diwali muhurat outside India

  • Americas, Europe, Asia-Pacific

    Same date everywhere

    From Auckland to Vancouver, the evening of Sunday 8 November is the Lakshmi Puja evening. Govardhan Puja moves to Monday 9 November in the western hemisphere.

  • Diaspora

    Use local sunset, not IST

    Converting 5:54 PM IST to your time zone gives the wrong answer. When it is 5:54 PM in Delhi it is early morning in North America.

  • Night puja

    Nishita in the Americas

    On the US and Canadian west coast, Amavasya ends late on 8 November, before Mahanishita, so the evening puja is the one to keep.

  • Australia, New Zealand

    Southern hemisphere

    In Sydney, Melbourne and Auckland the sky turns differently, so Simha lagna does not sit around midnight; the evening Vrishabha window is the one to use.

Diwali 2026 muhurat: questions people ask

Is Diwali 2026 on the same date in every country?

Yes. For every city on this page Lakshmi Puja falls on the evening of Sunday 8 November 2026, local time, because Amavasya covers that evening's Pradosh Kaal from New Zealand to the west coast of North America. Only the clock times change.

What is the Lakshmi Puja muhurat in Delhi for Diwali 2026?

5:54 PM – 7:50 PM IST on Sunday 8 November, the Vrishabha Kaal inside Pradosh Kaal (5:31 PM – 8:08 PM). This matches Drik Panchang's published 5:54 PM to 7:50 PM.

What is the Lakshmi Puja muhurat in the USA in 2026?

It depends on the city: New York 5:02 PM – 6:50 PM, Chicago 4:54 PM – 6:41 PM, Houston 5:51 PM – 7:46 PM, Los Angeles 5:14 PM – 7:06 PM and Seattle 4:56 PM – 6:38 PM, all local time on Sunday 8 November. Pick your city in the finder for the full set of timings.

Why is the muhurat different in every city?

Pradosh Kaal starts at local sunset, and Vrishabha lagna depends on the local horizon, so both move with latitude and longitude. Two cities in the same time zone can differ by half an hour or more.

What is Pradosh Kaal?

The first fifth of the night, counted from sunset to the next sunrise. It is the main window for Lakshmi Puja on Diwali.

What is Vrishabha Kaal and why does it matter?

The period when Vrishabha (Taurus) is rising on the eastern horizon. It is a sthir, or fixed, lagna, and tradition holds that Lakshmi stays in a home where she is worshipped during a fixed lagna, so the best muhurat is where Vrishabha Kaal overlaps Pradosh Kaal.

What is Mahanishita Kaal?

The muhurta at the middle of the night, used for Nishita puja, tantric worship and Kali Puja. In New Delhi it runs from about 11:38 PM to 12:31 AM. On the west coast of North America, Amavasya ends before Mahanishita, so families there usually finish the puja in the evening.

How accurate are these timings?

The calculator matches Drik Panchang's published Lakshmi Puja muhurat to the minute for New Delhi, Jaipur, Chennai and Pune, and is within two minutes for New York, Chicago and Los Angeles. Treat the first and last minute of any window as approximate, and follow your family priest or panchang if they differ.
