The 2026 Nobel Prizes: All Six Winners and What They Did

Optogenetics, ghost-particle astronomy, the chemistry of mirror-image molecules, a Canadian poet who rewrites the classics, a jurist for international law, and the economics of stabilising economies. Here is who won and why it matters.

By Prashant Dhingra · Updated November 17, 2026 · Announcements of October 5 to 12, 2026

The 2026 laureates

One card per prize, in the order announced.

MedicineOct 5

Physiology or Medicine: Deisseroth, Hegemann and Nagel

American Karl Deisseroth and Germans Peter Hegemann and Georg Nagel share the prize for discoveries on light-gated ion channels and optogenetics. The method lets researchers switch the activity of individual nerve cells in a living brain on or off, and labs around the world now use it to study how the brain works.

One-third share each

PhysicsOct 6

Physics: Francis Halzen

Belgian-American particle physicist Francis Halzen wins alone for decisive contributions to the IceCube Neutrino Observatory at the South Pole and the discovery of high-energy neutrinos from beyond our galaxy. The work established neutrino astronomy as a way to observe the universe alongside light, gravitational waves and cosmic rays.

Sole laureate

ChemistryOct 7

Chemistry: Henri Kagan and Kenso Soai

France's Henri Kagan, 95, and Japan's Kenso Soai are honoured for discovering non-linear effects and autocatalysis in asymmetric organic synthesis. Their work helps explain how life's one-handed chemistry could emerge, and it matters for making single mirror-image molecules in pharmaceuticals.

A century-old puzzle

LiteratureOct 8

Literature: Anne Carson

The Canadian poet, essayist and classicist, 76, is recognised for a bold and inventive body of work that, in playful dialogue with the classical tradition, created new forms for contemporary literature. Her writing blends verse, prose, dialogue and translation.

19 women among 122 laureates

PeaceOct 9

Peace: Navanethem "Navi" Pillay

The South African jurist and former UN human rights chief receives the 125th Peace Prize for her efforts to promote peace and international law. The Norwegian Nobel Committee said the prize is meant to remind the world that the rule of law underpins non-violent resolution of conflict.

125 years of the Peace Prize

EconomicsOct 12

Economic Sciences: Robert Hall and Michael Woodford

The two American-born economists are honoured for building macroeconomic models on microeconomic foundations. Their work, used by central banks and finance ministries, helps policymakers stabilise economies after shocks such as energy crises.

Only three women have ever won

Prize facts

Prize money

Each prize is worth 12 million Swedish kronor, about $1.2 million, shared when there are several laureates.

Ceremonies

The awards ceremonies are held on December 10, the anniversary of Alfred Nobel's death. The Peace Prize is presented in Oslo and the others in Stockholm.

The economics prize

It is not one of the five original prizes in Nobel's will. Sweden's central bank established it in 1968, and it was first awarded in 1969.

Gender balance

Only 19 women have won the Literature prize since 1901, and only three women have ever won the Economics prize.

Frequently asked questions

Who won the Nobel Prize in Physics 2026?

Francis Halzen won the 2026 Nobel Prize in Physics, announced on October 6, for decisive contributions to the IceCube Neutrino Observatory and the discovery of high-energy neutrinos of astrophysical origin.

Who won the Nobel Peace Prize 2026?

The 2026 Nobel Peace Prize went to South African jurist Navanethem "Navi" Pillay, announced on October 9 in Oslo, for her efforts to promote peace and international law.

Who won the Nobel Prize in Literature 2026?

Canadian poet and essayist Anne Carson won the 2026 Nobel Prize in Literature, announced on October 8, for a bold and inventive body of work that created new forms for contemporary literature in dialogue with the classical tradition.

Who won the Nobel Prize in Medicine 2026?

Karl Deisseroth, Peter Hegemann and Georg Nagel share the 2026 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine for discoveries concerning light-gated ion channels and optogenetics, announced on October 5.

Who won the Nobel Prize in Chemistry 2026?

Henri B. Kagan and Kenso Soai won the 2026 Nobel Prize in Chemistry for the discovery of non-linear effects and autocatalysis in asymmetric organic synthesis, announced on October 7.

Who won the Nobel Prize in Economics 2026?

Robert E. Hall and Michael Woodford won the 2026 Nobel Prize in Economic Sciences, announced on October 12, for work on the microeconomic foundations of macroeconomic stabilisation.

How much is the Nobel Prize worth and when is the ceremony?

Each 2026 prize is worth 12 million Swedish kronor, about $1.2 million, shared among laureates of the same prize. The ceremonies take place on December 10.
