How to Choose and Decorate Your Dandiya Sticks

Weight, material, and a DIY guide to wrapping sticks in gota patti and colorful bandhani fabric.

By Prashant Dhingra · Updated 7 October 2026

Read the guideAll garba and dandiya guides

On this page

  1. Choosing dandiya sticks
  2. DIY decoration
  3. Safety over ornament

Choosing dandiya sticks

FactorWhat to look for
Weightlight enough for repeated movement without wrist strain
Materialsmooth wood or well-finished alternatives; avoid splinters or sharp edges
Lengthcomfortable reach without forcing wide swings
Gripsecure but not bulky
Sounda clear strike without excessive force

DIY decoration

  1. Sand or inspect the sticks so the surface is smooth.
  2. Wrap with narrow bandhani fabric, ribbon or gota patti, keeping the grip section comfortable.
  3. Secure ends with strong adhesive or neatly stitched/knotted fabric.
  4. Add small tassels only if they cannot catch on other dancers.
  5. Test for loose mirrors, bells or sharp trim before the event.

Safety over ornament

Heavy metal decorations, protruding mirrors and long tassels can turn a dance prop into a hazard. Decorations should never interfere with grip or swing path.

Event and dance planning note

Garba and Dandiya work best when skill, crowd flow and safety are designed together. A beginner-friendly event needs space to learn, water access, clear entrances and exits, and music programming that does not stay at peak speed for the entire night.
