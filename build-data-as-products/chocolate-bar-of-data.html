The core shift

From Raw Data to
Decision Intelligence

Most enterprises stop at dashboards. DataKnobs goes further. Instead of merely displaying raw metrics, DataKnobs creates higher-level business concepts that humans can act on immediately.

Charts are not intelligence. Data stored in warehouses is not intelligence. Intelligence is what you can act on.

The fundamental shift DataKnobs enables is the movement up the value chain:

Data → Information → Intelligence → Action

Architecture philosophy

Four Layers from
Ingredients to Intelligence

DataKnobs is built around a simple but powerful principle: enterprises do not want raw data — they want consumable intelligence. The architecture reflects this in four progressive layers.

01
Foundation
Raw Data Foundation
Sensors APIs Databases Documents Streams External feeds Market systems
02
Enrichment
Semantic Enrichment
Meaning Relationships Trends Context Anomalies
03
Intelligence
Composite Intelligence

This creates reusable enterprise intelligence products that compound in value across teams and decisions.

Why it matters

Three Problems
DataKnobs Solves

01
Too Much Data
Companies collect enormous amounts of information but struggle to convert it into decisions. Volume without structure is not an asset.
02
Fragmented Systems
Operational data, financial data, and customer data live in silos. The connections that create insight don't happen automatically.
03
Human Bottlenecks
Experts spend countless hours interpreting raw metrics manually. DataKnobs creates AI-driven abstraction layers that free this time.
