Frequently asked questions

What does this Azure generative AI presentation cover?

The five-slide deck maps the Azure generative AI stack across experiences, governance, agents, knowledge and tools, models and infrastructure.

How does the deck organize an agent application?

It separates runtime and agent services, retrieval through Foundry IQ and Azure AI Search, model access through Foundry Models, and governance through guardrails, policy, identity and observability.

Which entry points does the presentation compare?

The source deck compares Copilot Studio for low-code business applications, Microsoft Foundry for custom development, and an approach that combines both.

Are the model lists and product dates independently verified here?

No. This page reproduces the supplied November 2026 presentation. Model availability, product names, pricing and lifecycle dates are source-deck claims and should be checked against Azure documentation before implementation.
