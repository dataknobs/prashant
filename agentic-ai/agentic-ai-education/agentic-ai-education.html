Inside the course tutor agent

The flagship workflow connects evidence gathering to a defined action, with a person at a critical checkpoint. The sequence below summarizes the source slide.

  1. Read the syllabus and approved course materials
  2. Assess understanding and identify a weak topic
  3. Provide targeted practice and explain errors
  4. Flag students who need teacher support
  5. Report progress to the learner and teacher

Questions answered

Where should education teams start?

Student-services Q&A and course-design assistance. Use a bounded pilot with a baseline and a named owner.

What is the featured workflow?

Course tutor agent. Report progress to the learner and teacher.

How is value measured?

Learning gains, Course completion, Teacher time, Student-support response time. Compare benefits with error rates, review effort and operating cost.

What does autonomous mean in this deck?

The agent acts within set limits while humans monitor. It does not mean unlimited authority or an absence of oversight.
