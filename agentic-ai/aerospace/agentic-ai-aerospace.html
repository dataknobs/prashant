Inside the aircraft maintenance agent

The flagship workflow connects evidence gathering to a defined action, with a person at a critical checkpoint. The sequence below summarizes the source slide.

  1. Read sensor trends and fault codes
  2. Match evidence to manuals and previous repairs
  3. Draft a work package and parts list
  4. Check inventory and maintenance slots
  5. Have a certified engineer review and sign off

A practical roadmap

Questions answered

Where should aerospace and defence teams start?

Technical-document assistance and cyber-alert triage. Use a bounded pilot with a baseline and a named owner.

What is the featured workflow?

Aircraft maintenance agent. Have a certified engineer review and sign off.

How is value measured?

Aircraft availability, Unscheduled removals, Maintenance turnaround time, Analyst throughput. Compare benefits with error rates, review effort and operating cost.

What does autonomous mean in this deck?

The agent acts within set limits while humans monitor. It does not mean unlimited authority or an absence of oversight.
