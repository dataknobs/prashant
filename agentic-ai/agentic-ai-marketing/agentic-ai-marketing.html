Six use cases and autonomy levels

Inside the weekly campaign agent

The flagship workflow connects evidence gathering to a defined action, with a person at a critical checkpoint. The sequence below summarizes the source slide.

  1. Collect campaign results from ad platforms and CRM
  2. Analyze performance and possible drivers
  3. Propose budget adjustments within configured limits
  4. Obtain marketer approval
  5. Apply approved changes and measure results

A practical roadmap

Start with automated performance reports and reviewed content drafts. Capped budget optimization and competitive monitoring, followed by evaluated retention actions and SEO support. The deck’s 90-day, 3–12-month and longer-term stages are planning suggestions, not delivery guarantees.

Questions answered

Where should marketing teams start?

Automated performance reports and reviewed content drafts. Use a bounded pilot with a baseline and a named owner.

What is the featured workflow?

Weekly campaign agent. Apply approved changes and measure results.

How is value measured?

Return on ad spend, Time to insight, Content cycle time, Conversion rate. Compare benefits with error rates, review effort and operating cost.

What does autonomous mean in this deck?

The agent acts within set limits while humans monitor. It does not mean unlimited authority or an absence of oversight.
