Inside the onboarding agent

The flagship workflow connects evidence gathering to a defined action, with a person at a critical checkpoint. The sequence below summarizes the source slide.

  1. Create a role-specific onboarding plan
  2. Request accounts, equipment and appropriate access
  3. Guide the new hire through forms and training
  4. Answer questions from policy documents
  5. Have the manager confirm first-week readiness

Questions answered

Where should human resources teams start?

Policy and benefits Q&A plus candidate scheduling. Use a bounded pilot with a baseline and a named owner.

What is the featured workflow?

Onboarding agent. Have the manager confirm first-week readiness.

How is value measured?

Time to hire, Candidate experience, Time to productivity, HR ticket volume. Compare benefits with error rates, review effort and operating cost.

What does autonomous mean in this deck?

The agent acts within set limits while humans monitor. It does not mean unlimited authority or an absence of oversight.
