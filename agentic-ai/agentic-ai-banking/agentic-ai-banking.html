Inside the fraud investigation agent

The flagship workflow connects evidence gathering to a defined action, with a person at a critical checkpoint. The sequence below summarizes the source slide.

  1. Pull account, card and transaction history
  2. Check device, location and merchant signals
  3. Compare patterns with previous cases
  4. Draft a case summary and proposed action
  5. Have an analyst approve a block, refund or release

Questions answered

Where should banking and finance teams start?

Routine customer queries and adviser meeting briefs. Use a bounded pilot with a baseline and a named owner.

What is the featured workflow?

Fraud investigation agent. Have an analyst approve a block, refund or release.

How is value measured?

Cost per contact, Containment rate, Alert handling time, False-positive rate. Compare benefits with error rates, review effort and operating cost.

What does autonomous mean in this deck?

The agent acts within set limits while humans monitor. It does not mean unlimited authority or an absence of oversight.
