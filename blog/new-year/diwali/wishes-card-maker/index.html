How it works

  1. Choose a design and colors. Four layouts, each redrawn live with your words. The photo layout puts your own picture on the card.
  2. Pick or write a wish. Browse wishes by who you're sending to, switch between English and Hindi, or type your own.
  3. Download or send a link. Save the card for WhatsApp or Instagram, or send a link that opens a full-screen greeting with the names you added.

Questions

Is the New Year card maker free?

Yes. Make and download as many cards as you like. There is no sign-up and no watermark beyond a small site credit.

How does the wish link work?

The link carries the names and the wish you chose. When your friend opens it, they see a full-screen fireworks greeting with their name and a button to send one back.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Photos you add to the photo design stay on your device and are only used to draw the card in your browser. They are never part of the wish link.

What size are the cards?

Square cards are 1080 × 1080 pixels for WhatsApp chats and Instagram posts. Story cards are 1080 × 1920 for WhatsApp Status and Instagram Stories.

Can I send New Year wishes in Hindi?

Yes. Switch the language to हिन्दी to pick a Hindi wish, or type your own message in any language.

What day is New Year 2027?

New Year's Day 2027 falls on Friday, January 1. New Year's Eve is Thursday, December 31, 2026.
