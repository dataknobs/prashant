    Party games

    Resolution roulette

    6+ players, about 15 min

    Everyone writes a resolution anonymously on a slip. The host reads them out one by one and the room guesses who wrote each.

    The year in 60 seconds

    2 teams players, about 20 min

    Teams act out moments from the past year, charades style. One minute per turn, no words allowed.

    Midnight time capsule

    Everyone players, about 10 min

    Each guest writes three predictions for next year. Seal them in an envelope and open it at next year's party.

    Toast relay

    6+ players, about 10 min

    Go round the room with one-line toasts. Each new toast must begin with the last word of the one before.

    Countdown hot potato

    6+ players, about 10 min

    Pass an object while music plays. When it stops, the holder answers a trivia question or shares their best moment of the year.

    Tambola

    4+ players, about 30 min

    Hand out tickets and use the number caller below. First to complete a line, a full house or an agreed pattern calls it out.

    Questions

    How many drinks do I need for a New Year's Eve party?

    A common rule of thumb is two drinks per guest in the first hour and one per guest for every hour after that. For a five-hour party with 20 guests, that is 20 × (2 + 4) = 120 drinks, plus a glass each for the midnight toast.

    How much ice should I buy?

    Plan on about 0.5 kg (1 lb) of ice per guest, and more if you are chilling bottles in tubs rather than a fridge.

    Will the countdown work in my time zone?

    Yes. The countdown uses the clock on the device showing it, so it reaches zero at midnight wherever you are. Check that the device's time is set automatically.

    Can I show the countdown on a TV?

    Open the page on a laptop connected to the TV, or in the TV's browser, and press Full screen. The page asks the device to keep the screen awake while it runs.

    Are the trivia answers shown to players?

    No. The host controls when each answer is revealed, and the options are shuffled every time you start a new game.

    What is tambola?

    Tambola, also called housie, is a bingo-style number game popular at parties in India. Players mark numbers on printed tickets as the caller draws 1 to 90.
