      How to keep them past February

      Make it specific.

      "Walk 8,000 steps on five days a week" is easier to keep than "get fit", because you know on any given day whether you did it.

      Start with a first step.

      Choose something you can do this week, like buying running shoes or setting up an automatic transfer.

      Keep the list short.

      Two or three resolutions you care about beat ten you half-remember.

      Check in, don't start over.

      A missed week is a data point, not a failure. The monthly check-in is there to adjust the plan.

      Questions

      How many resolutions should I make?

      Fewer than you think. Most people do better with one to three resolutions they care about than a long list. The builder lets you add up to five.

      What makes a resolution easier to keep?

      Make it specific, give it a first step you can take this week, and decide how often you'll do it. Writing down why it matters helps on the days motivation runs low.

      How do the calendar reminders work?

      Download the .ics file and open it. Your calendar app (Google, Apple, Outlook) adds a check-in on the first of every month from February to December 2027, listing your resolutions.

      How does the letter to my future self get delivered?

      It becomes a calendar event on December 31, 2027, with your letter in the event notes. Add it to your calendar and it will be waiting for you. The letter never leaves your device.

      Is my information saved?

      Your answers, resolutions and tracker ticks are saved in this browser only, so you can come back to them. Nothing is sent anywhere.

      Can I share my resolutions?

      Yes. Download the resolutions card or the year-in-review card for WhatsApp or Instagram, as a square post or a story.
