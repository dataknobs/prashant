    How to print your 2027 calendar

    1. Choose a layout. Full year fits all twelve months and the holiday list on one sheet. Month by month gives each month its own page with space to write.
    2. Pick your holidays and paper. Letter is standard in the US and Canada; A4 everywhere else. The week start and paper size switch to the local default when you pick a country, and you can change either one.
    3. Add your own dates. Birthdays and deadlines appear in blue on both layouts.
    4. Print. Use Print this month for a single page or Print all 12 months for the set. In the print dialog, keep scale at 100% and turn off headers and footers for a clean edge.
    5. Save as PDF instead, if you like. Choose "Save as PDF" as the printer to keep a copy for your phone or a print shop.

    Questions about the 2027 calendar

    Is this 2027 calendar free to print?

    Yes. Print as many copies as you like for home, school or office use. Sharing the page link is welcome; please don't resell the printouts.

    What day of the week does 2027 start on?

    2027 starts on Friday, January 1, and ends on Friday, December 31. Because it starts on a Friday and is not a leap year, Friday occurs 53 times.

    Is 2027 a leap year?

    No. 2027 has 365 days and February has 28 days. The next leap year is 2028.

    Which years have the same calendar as 2027?

    2021 and 2010 match 2027 exactly, weekday for weekday. The next year that reuses it is 2038.

    How many US federal holidays are there in 2027?

    There are 11 federal holidays. Juneteenth, Independence Day and Christmas fall on weekends, so they are observed on June 18, July 5 and December 24. New Year's Day 2028 falls on a Saturday, so it is observed on Friday, December 31, 2027.

    What are the UK bank holidays in 2027?

    England and Wales have eight bank holidays in 2027. Christmas Day falls on a Saturday and Boxing Day on a Sunday, so substitute bank holidays are Monday, 27 December and Tuesday, 28 December.

    When are Diwali, Holi and Eid in 2027?

    According to India's central government holiday list, Diwali is on Friday, October 29, Holi on Tuesday, March 23, and Id-ul-Fitr on Wednesday, March 10, 2027. Islamic festival dates depend on moon sighting and may shift by a day.

    When is Easter 2027?

    Easter Sunday is March 28, 2027. Good Friday is March 26 and Easter Monday is March 29.

    How do I print on A4 or Letter paper?

    Choose A4 or Letter in the Paper setting before you print, then keep scale at 100% in the print dialog. The page size is sent to your printer automatically.

    Can I save the calendar as a PDF?

    Yes. Click Print and choose Save as PDF as the destination. This works in Chrome, Edge, Safari and Firefox.
