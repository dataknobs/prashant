LLM Guardrails for Compliance

Regulator-focused framework for safe, auditable AI systems

Why Regulators Care

LLMs introduce risks including data leakage, bias, hallucinations, and lack of auditability. A structured guardrail system ensures compliance with legal, privacy, and governance obligations.

Guardrail Architecture

Policy & Governance

Define approved use cases, prohibited behaviors, and compliance ownership.

Data Controls

Classify, mask, and restrict sensitive data before model access.

Input Controls

Detect prompt injection, unsafe intent, and policy violations.

Model Controls

Restrict tools, enforce templates, and limit responses to trusted sources.

Output Controls

Filter bias, toxicity, hallucinations, and compliance risks.

Human Oversight

Require human review for regulated or high-risk decisions.

Regulatory Operating Model

Prevent

Control inputs, data, and access upfront.

Detect

Monitor real-time activity and policy violations.

Respond

Escalate and remediate incidents with accountability.

Audit

Maintain logs and evidence for regulators.

What Regulators Expect

  • Documented AI use cases and risk classification
  • Clear policies for data handling and model usage
  • Tested guardrails with measurable effectiveness
  • Audit logs and explainability
  • Incident response processes
