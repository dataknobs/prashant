Executive Overview & Context

This interactive dashboard translates the core findings of the report on AI and traditional data management. The industry is currently facing a profound dual reality. On one side, legacy models based on rigid schemas and manual plumbing are under siege by agile, AI-native alternatives. On the other side, incumbents possess massive "Data Gravity" the essential fuel required to make AI work in the enterprise.

💡 Strategic Takeaway

The Ultimate Conclusion: Data Gravity

The AI era is an "adapt or perish" moment. Companies clinging to legacy licensing and manual paradigms will be outmaneuvered. However, incumbents hold the ultimate trump card: Data Gravity. Because they already house the world's most valuable enterprise data, if they successfully embed AI into their core engines to automate, democratize, and secure data, they will not only survive the revolution they will power it.
