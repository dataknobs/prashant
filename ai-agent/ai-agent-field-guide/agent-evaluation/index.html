Evaluating AI Agents

How to evaluate an agent at the task, trajectory, tool-use, safety, and business-outcome levels.

Key ideas
  • Evaluate outcomes and trajectories
  • Core evaluation dimensions
  • Offline and online
  • Business success matters

Evaluate outcomes and trajectories

A final answer can look correct even when the agent took unsafe or wasteful steps. Score both what happened and how the agent got there.

Core evaluation dimensions

Measure task success, factuality, tool selection, tool argument correctness, policy compliance, handoff quality, recovery behavior, latency, cost, and escalation quality.

Offline and online

Use curated test suites and simulations before release, then production traces, sampled review, and business KPIs after deployment.

Business success matters

Technical success is insufficient. Tie agent metrics to cycle time, quality, revenue, cost, risk, satisfaction, or another explicit business outcome.
