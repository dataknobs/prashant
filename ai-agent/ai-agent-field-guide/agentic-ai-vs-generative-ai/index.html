Agentic AI vs. Generative AI

Understand the difference between content generation and goal-directed action, and when each pattern fits.

Key ideas
  • Generative AI creates; agentic AI acts
  • The control difference
  • The risk difference

Generative AI creates; agentic AI acts

Generative AI primarily produces content in response to a prompt. Agentic AI uses generation as one component inside a workflow that chooses actions, interacts with systems, and pursues a goal.

The control difference

A chatbot usually waits for the next user turn. An agent may continue across multiple steps, invoke tools, inspect results, and re-plan until a completion or escalation condition is reached.

The risk difference

Once a model can take actions, reliability is no longer only about answer quality. Identity, authorization, side effects, rollback, observability, and human control become first-class design concerns.
