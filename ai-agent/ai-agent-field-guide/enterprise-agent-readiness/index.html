Enterprise Readiness for Agentic AI

Assess whether your organization is ready to deploy agents beyond demos and pilots.

Key ideas
  • Strategy readiness
  • Data readiness
  • Tool readiness
  • Governance readiness

Strategy readiness

There should be a business problem, accountable sponsor, measurable outcome, and realistic autonomy target.

Data readiness

Agents need reliable access to the data required for decisions, with permissions, lineage, quality controls, and clear system-of-record boundaries.

Tool readiness

Enterprise actions should be exposed through stable, narrow, observable interfaces instead of brittle UI automation wherever possible.

Governance readiness

Identity, authorization, policy, audit, incident response, and human escalation must exist before meaningful autonomy.

Operating readiness

Teams need ownership for production traces, evaluation regressions, costs, model changes, and tool/API changes.

People readiness

The workflow and human roles often need redesign. Adoption is an organizational transformation, not merely a software deployment.
