AI Agent Frameworks

A practical way to choose among graph-based, role-based, event-driven, type-safe, retrieval-focused, and full-stack agent frameworks.

Key ideas
  • Choose the abstraction, not the brand
  • Graph/state-machine frameworks
  • Role/conversation frameworks
  • Type-safe frameworks

Choose the abstraction, not the brand

Framework selection should follow your control-flow, durability, language, deployment, and governance requirements.

Graph/state-machine frameworks

Best when workflows need explicit states, checkpoints, conditional routing, auditability, and controlled cycles.

Role/conversation frameworks

Useful for rapid multi-agent prototyping and specialist collaboration, but they can become harder to reason about as autonomy grows.

Type-safe frameworks

Helpful when schema validation, dependency injection, structured outputs, and integration with existing typed systems are central.

Retrieval-first workflows

Strong when the agent’s primary job is knowledge retrieval, synthesis, and source-grounded reasoning.

Production filter

Evaluate persistence, retries, human approval support, tracing, identity integration, deployment model, testability, and cost—not just demo speed.
