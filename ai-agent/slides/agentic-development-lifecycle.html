Agent Engineering

The Agentic Development Lifecycle

Traditional software ships once and waits for the next release. An AI agent keeps reasoning, calling tools, and making decisions on its own — which means the way teams plan, build, and run it has to change too. The Agentic Development Lifecycle (ADLC) is the closed loop that most teams converge on: six recurring stages that take an agent from idea to production, and feed what happens in production back into the next iteration.
